Preface

This textbook, History for Secondary Schools, is written specifically for Form One students in the United Republic of Tanzania. The book is prepared in accordance with the 2023 History Syllabus for Ordinary Secondary Education, Form I-IV, issued by the Ministry of Education Science and Technology. It is a revised edition of History for Secondary Schools Student’s Book for Form One published in 2019 in accordance with the 2005 syllabus issued by the then, Ministry of Education and Vocational Training (MoEVT).

The book consists of seven (7) chapters, namely Introduction to History, Origin of human beings, Production and social organisation in pre-colonial Africa and Development of agriculture in pre-colonial African societies. Others are Handcraft and mining industries in pre-colonial Africa, Social, political and education systems in pre-colonial Africa and State organisation in pre-colonial Africa. In addition to the contents, the book contains illustrations, activities and exercises. You are encouraged to do all the activities, exercises, and other assignments your teacher will provide. You are also required to prepare a portfolio for keeping records of activities performed in different lessons. Doing so will enable you to develop the intended competencies.

Additional learning resources are available at TIE e-Library https://ol.tie.go.tz or ol.tie.go.tz
