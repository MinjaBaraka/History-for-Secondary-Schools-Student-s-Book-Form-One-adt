Glossary

Abolition official ending of a law, system or practice
Acheulian type of tool produced from large flakes of stone during the early stone age
Age-set group of people initiated at the same time, mainly in some of the pastoral societies
Ancestor people from previous generations in a family such as grand parents, great-grand parents, or even more distant relatives
Archaeology study of past cultures by examining their remnants found on or under the ground
Artifact simple object (such as a tool or ornament) showing human workmanship or modification as distinguished from a natural object
Arson crime of intentionally starting a fire to damage or destroy something especially buildings
Centralised state system of governance or political organisation in which political power is centrally exercised by the chief, king or president
Century period of one hundred years
Chief leader of an ethnic group consisting of several clans
Clan several families that have a common ancestor
Commodity product that can be bought or sold
Community group of people who relate to one another closely in the day-to-day living
Conflict disagreement that sometimes leads to misunderstanding
Coronation ceremony at which a person is made a chief, king or queen
Cranial capacity size of brain
Creation making or causing the existence something new
